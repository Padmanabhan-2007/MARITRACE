## 1. Executive Summary

In adherence to ISO 27037 standards for digital evidence handling and responsible AI guidelines, this report explicitly documents all unavailable datasets, missing ground-truth labels, sensor limitations, timeline validations, and physical model assumptions present in the MARITRACE pipeline. 

MARITRACE operates under a strict principle of **scientific humility**: where data is incomplete, corrupt, unverified, or temporally unaligned, the system must **abstain** or request corroborating evidence rather than fabricating certainty or prematurely declaring vessel culpability.

## 2. Dataset Integrity & Model Evaluation Rigor

### 2.1 Ocean-Current NetCDF File Corruption
- **Inspected Asset:** `cmems_obs-mob_glo_phy-cur_nrt_0.25deg_P1D-m_1792237857475.nc`
- **Observed State:** File size is strictly **0 bytes** on disk. No NetCDF-4 binary header exists.
- **Root Cause:** Incomplete download or truncated network transfer prior to offline archival.
- **System Handling:** The validation engine (`OceanDriftEngine.validate_netcdf`) explicitly detects the 0-byte state, records the corruption in the tamper-evident audit log, and safely activates calibrated regional hydrodynamic flow fields (Gulf of Mexico Loop Current mean vectors: $u = +0.18\,\text{m/s}$, $v = -0.08\,\text{m/s}$; wind $u = -3.5\,\text{m/s}$, $v = -2.0\,\text{m/s}$ with 12° Coriolis deflection).
- **Mandatory Reporting Label:** All resulting drift envelopes are strictly labeled as **Scenario-Based Drift Estimates (Regional Fallback Parameters)** rather than validated real-time physical measurements.

### 2.2 Satellite SAR Spatial-Block Holdout (Not Scene-Level Validation)
- **Asset:** CSIRO Sentinel-1 SAR Dataset (`Class_0`: 3,695 chips; `Class_1`: 1,843 chips).
- **Spatial Autocorrelation Challenge:** Consecutive image chips extracted from the same satellite acquisition track exhibit spatial autocorrelation (mean pixel intensity difference between consecutive chips is 51.4 vs 60.7 for distant chips). A naive random split could theoretically suffer from intra-scene texture leakage.
- **Available benchmark evidence:**
  1. **Stratified image split (831 Test Chips):**
     - Accuracy: **94.83%** | Precision: **89.80%** | Recall: **95.31%** | F1 (Oil): **0.9247** | ROC-AUC: **0.9857**
  2. **Spatial-block holdout (831 chips; see `runs/prod_outputs/models/satellite_classifier/spatial_block_manifest.json`):**
     - Accuracy: **96.39%** | Precision: **91.86%** | Recall: **97.83%** | F1 (Oil): **0.9476** | ROC-AUC: **0.9970**
- **Interpretation:** The spatial-block score is a spatial-block holdout result only. The available manifest records filename ranges, not acquisition IDs, so it does not establish generalization to an unseen acquisition or scene. The earlier "scene-level" wording and generalization conclusion are withdrawn. `scene_manifest.csv` with an explicit filename-to-acquisition mapping is now supported by the split builder; training must be rerun on that manifest before claiming scene-level validation. New split manifests include file hashes.

### 2.3 Absence of Adjudicated Ground-Truth Polluter Labels
- **Inspected Asset:** `incidents.csv` (NOAA Office of Response and Restoration Emergency Response Division)
- **Observed State:** Contains 4,946 historical incident records describing spill dates, coordinates, threats, and observational narratives.
- **Limitation:** The database does **not** provide verified legal determinations of which specific vessel caused the discharge.
- **System Handling:** The pipeline strictly avoids treating incident records as ground-truth vessel liability labels. Suspect rankings generated by the fusion engine represent investigative hypotheses, not adjudicated guilt.

## 3. Timeline Alignment & Spatiotemporal Incompatibilities

### 3.1 Critical Timeline Alignment Validation (NOAA Incident 10716 vs AIS 2024-01-02)
- **NOAA Incident 10716:** Opened on **January 7, 2024** (*Mystery Sheen; vicinity of Coquille Bay, LA* at 29.1350° N, -89.3039° W).
- **Available AIS Tracking Snapshot:** Single-day observation file `ais-2024-01-02.csv.zst` recorded on **January 2, 2024** (5 days prior).
- **Physical Interpretation:**
  - The vessels tracked on January 2 (e.g., `268 BOOSTER`, `JACKE M`, `MISS GWEN`) were operating in the exact Mississippi Delta shipping channel, but 5 days prior to the reported discharge.
  - Therefore, the candidate vessels tracked in MARITRACE represent a **realistic regional historical traffic baseline scenario**, NOT eyewitness observations of the January 7 spill.
- **Defensible System Behavior:** January 2 AIS is five days before the January 7 incident. The corrected fusion engine returns `ABSTAIN`, emits zero incident-specific ranked leads, and retains those tracks only in `historical_baseline_vessels`.

## 4. Vessel Classification Scope & AIS Tracking Limitations

### 4.1 Scope of Vessel Machine Learning Model
- **Task:** The trained `RandomForestClassifier` (60.40% Accuracy, 0.4801 Macro-F1 across 6 gear categories) predicts **vessel gear and operational type** (`drifting_longlines`, `fixed_gear`, `pole_and_line`, `purse_seines`, `trawlers`, `trollers`).
- **Critical Clarification:** The model classifies vessel operational behavior; **it does NOT predict whether a vessel caused an oil spill.** A vessel classified as a trawler or cargo carrier simply receives a contextual profile based on fuel capacity, not a presumption of guilt.

### 4.2 Terrestrial AIS Coverage Gaps vs. Deliberate Blackouts
- **Phenomenon:** Terrestrial VHF AIS receivers have line-of-sight propagation constraints (typically 20 to 40 nautical miles). Atmospheric ducting, vessel antenna shadow, receiver port congestion, and coastal topography frequently cause multi-hour signal dropouts.
- **Forensic Rule:** **Missing AIS data must NEVER automatically be interpreted as suspicious or evasive behavior.**
- **Mitigation:** The Evidence Sufficiency engine recommends querying commercial satellite-AIS archives (e.g., Spire Maritime, exactEarth) to determine if a vessel transponder was truly deactivated or merely beyond terrestrial antenna range.

## 5. Next-Best-Evidence & Uncertainty Reduction Formulation

### 5.1 Heuristic Uncertainty Index (Not Bayesian)
Uncertainty is a heuristic decision-support index from $0.0$ (lower) to $1.0$ (higher). It is not a calibrated probability, Bayesian posterior, or empirically measured information gain.

- **Prior Incident Uncertainty ($U_0$):**
  - For `ABSTAIN` incidents: $U_0 = 0.85$
  - For `AMBIGUOUS_LEADS` incidents: $U_0 = 0.65$
  - For single candidate leads: $U_0 = \max(0.15, 1.0 - \text{Score})$
- **Illustrative acquisition decrement ($d$):** modality values are policy heuristics, not measured gains.
- Projected index is $U_1 = \max(0.05, U_0 - d)$. Relative decrease is $(U_0-U_1)/U_0$.
- For satellite-AIS, $U_0=0.65$, $d=0.35$, and $U_1=0.30$: this is a 0.35 absolute index decrement and a 53.8% relative decrease.

## 6. Cryptographic Audit Trail Boundaries

- **Capability:** The SHA-256 hash-chain audit log (`AuditTrail`) guarantees that every logged event (ingested file hash, model checkpoint hash, decision score, and abstention reason) is tamper-evident and cannot be modified post-hoc without breaking the cryptographic hash chain.
- **Fundamental Boundary:** A cryptographic hash chain proves that a log entry was recorded at a given time and has not been altered; **it does not prove that the underlying sensor data was accurate, untampered at the sensor head, or truthful.** Upstream sensor validation requires multi-modal cross-corroboration.

## 7. Hardcoded Abstention Criteria Summary

The system is hardcoded to emit an explicit `ABSTAIN` or `AMBIGUOUS_LEADS` decision under any of the following four conditions:
1. **Low SAR Confidence ($P_{\text{oil}} < 0.50$):** Image classified as oceanic look-alike (wind calm, biogenic film, or algae slick).
2. **Spatial Disconnect ($d_{\text{min}} > 35\,\text{km}$):** No candidate vessels observed inside or near the physical drift origin envelope.
3. **Inconclusive Corroboration ($\text{Score} < 0.40$):** Weak spatial/temporal alignment without supporting vessel contextual risk.
4. **Candidate Ambiguity ($|\Delta \text{Score}| < 0.04$):** Multiple candidate vessels exhibit indistinguishable likelihood scores; the system issues an `AMBIGUOUS_LEADS` alert and triggers the Next-Best-Evidence engine to recommend discriminating acquisitions.
