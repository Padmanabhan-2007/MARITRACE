# MARITRACE System Limitations and Assumptions Report

**Document Version:** 1.0.0  
**Project:** MARITRACE (Maritime Pollution Forensics and Evidence Fusion)  
**System Audit Date:** 2026-10-17  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

In adherence to ISO 27037 standards for digital evidence handling and responsible AI guidelines, this report explicitly documents all unavailable datasets, missing ground-truth labels, sensor limitations, and physical model assumptions present in the MARITRACE pipeline. 

MARITRACE operates under a strict principle of **scientific humility**: where data is incomplete, corrupt, or unverified, the system must **abstain** or request corroborating evidence rather than fabricating certainty or prematurely declaring vessel culpability.

## 2. Dataset Integrity & Missing Modalities

### 2.1 Ocean-Current NetCDF File Corruption
- **Inspected Asset:** `cmems_obs-mob_glo_phy-cur_nrt_0.25deg_P1D-m_1792237857475.nc`
- **Observed State:** File size is strictly **0 bytes** on disk. No NetCDF-4 binary header exists.
- **Root Cause:** Incomplete download or truncated network transfer prior to offline archival.
- **System Handling:** The validation engine (`OceanDriftEngine.validate_netcdf`) explicitly detects the 0-byte state, records the corruption in the tamper-evident audit log, and safely activates calibrated regional hydrodynamic flow fields (Gulf of Mexico Loop Current mean vectors: $u = +0.18\,\text{m/s}$, $v = -0.08\,\text{m/s}$) with clear provenance disclosure.
- **Operational Requirement:** High-resolution baroclinic hydrodynamic models must be re-ingested before deploying drift origin envelopes in formal legal proceedings.

### 2.2 Absence of Adjudicated Ground-Truth Polluter Labels
- **Inspected Asset:** `incidents.csv` (NOAA Office of Response and Restoration Emergency Response Division)
- **Observed State:** Contains 4,946 historical incident records describing spill dates, coordinates, threats, and observational narratives (e.g., Incident 10716, *Mystery Sheen; vicinity of Coquille Bay, LA*).
- **Limitation:** The database does **not** provide verified legal determinations of which specific vessel caused the discharge.
- **System Handling:** The pipeline strictly avoids treating incident records as ground-truth vessel liability labels. Suspect rankings generated by the fusion engine represent investigative hypotheses, not adjudicated guilt.

**Policy Enforcement:** Datasets are never merged merely because they exist in the same local directory. Strict schema normalization and spatiotemporal boundary gates prevent cross-domain contamination.

## 4. Sensor & Tracking Limitations

### 4.1 Terrestrial AIS Coverage Gaps vs. Deliberate Blackouts
- **Phenomenon:** Terrestrial VHF AIS receivers have line-of-sight propagation constraints (typically 20 to 40 nautical miles). Atmospheric ducting, vessel antenna shadow, receiver port congestion, and coastal topography frequently cause multi-hour signal dropouts.
- **Critical Forensic Rule:** **Missing AIS data must NEVER automatically be interpreted as suspicious or evasive behavior.**
- **Mitigation:** The Evidence Sufficiency engine recommends querying commercial satellite-AIS archives (e.g., Spire Maritime, exactEarth) to determine if a vessel transponder was truly deactivated or merely beyond terrestrial antenna range.

### 4.2 Vessel Gear Benchmark Anonymization
- **Asset:** Global Fishing Watch CSVs (`drifting_longlines.csv`, `fixed_gear.csv`, etc.)
- **Limitation:** MMSI identifiers in the benchmark dataset are anonymized floats/integers, and over 90% of individual GPS pings have unverified fishing activity (`is_fishing = -1.0`).
- **Mitigation:** Machine learning models are trained only on verified gear categories with strict `GroupShuffleSplit` on vessel IDs, ensuring models evaluate generalization across unseen vessels rather than memorizing specific ship IDs.

## 5. Physical Drift Model Assumptions

The MARITRACE ocean drift engine implements a Lagrangian advection model with the following physical assumptions:
1. **Empirical Wind Factor:** Applied at 3.0% of the 10-meter wind speed ($W_{10}$), standard for open-ocean surface slick drift.
2. **Coriolis Deflection:** Surface drift is deflected 12° to the right of the wind vector in the Northern Hemisphere (Ekman transport approximation).
3. **Turbulent Eddy Diffusivity:** Parameterized as $K_h = 15.0\,\text{m}^2/\text{s}$, producing an expanding uncertainty envelope ($\sim 2 \sigma$) over hindcast time steps.
4. **Unmodeled Weathering Processes:** The current engine does not simulate oil evaporation, emulsification ("chocolate mousse" formation), vertical turbulent entrainment, or shoreline stranding kinetics. Backward origin envelopes represent probable geometric search cones, not exact hydrodynamic trajectories.

## 6. Cryptographic Audit Trail Boundaries

- **Capability:** The SHA-256 hash-chain audit log (`AuditTrail`) guarantees that every logged event (ingested file hash, model checkpoint hash, decision score, and abstention reason) is tamper-evident and cannot be modified post-hoc without breaking the cryptographic hash chain.
- **Fundamental Boundary:** A cryptographic hash chain proves that a log entry was recorded at a given time and has not been altered; **it does not prove that the underlying sensor data was accurate, untampered at the sensor head, or truthful.** Upstream sensor validation requires multi-modal cross-corroboration.

## 7. Abstention Criteria Summary

The system is hardcoded to emit an explicit `ABSTAIN` decision under any of the following four conditions:
1. **Low SAR Confidence ($P_{\text{oil}} < 0.50$):** Image classified as oceanic look-alike (wind calm, biogenic film, or algae slick).
2. **Spatial Disconnect ($d_{\text{min}} > 35\,\text{km}$):** No candidate vessels observed inside or near the physical drift origin envelope.
3. **Inconclusive Corroboration ($\text{Score} < 0.40$):** Weak spatial/temporal alignment without supporting vessel contextual risk.
4. **Candidate Ambiguity ($|\Delta \text{Score}| < 0.04$):** Multiple candidate vessels exhibit indistinguishable likelihood scores; the system issues an `AMBIGUOUS_LEADS` alert and triggers the Next-Best-Evidence engine to recommend discriminating acquisitions.
